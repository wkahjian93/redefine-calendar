# REDEFINE Calendar: how to use

**The problem it fixes:** you had to ask each agent one by one when they're busy and what they're doing. Now everyone's schedule and activity is on one calendar you can open any time, and private things stay private.

## First time (every member, 1 minute)
1. Open the app link. On iPhone: Safari → Share ⬆︎ → **Add to Home Screen**. On Android: Chrome ⋮ → **Install app**.
2. Type the **team code**, your **name** and a **4–6 digit PIN**. Leaders use the **leader code** instead.
3. **Me → Turn on notifications** (iPhone: open the app from the home screen first).
4. **Me → Import calendar** to move your old appointments in (see below).

You stay signed in on that phone for good, like WhatsApp. Only two things sign you out: tapping **Sign out** on that phone, or a leader removing you. Changing the team code or losing signal never does.

## Every day (agents)
- **Add an appointment:** tap **+**, type what it is ("Present Mr Lim medical card", "签单 Ooi", "FU Jason"). The app works out the type (👋 Approach, ☕ Open, 📊 Present, 🔁 Follow-up, ✍️ Closing, 🌱 Recruit, 👥 Meeting, 🛠️ Service, 🎁 CRM, 📝 Paperwork, 🏠 Personal). Tap another type if it guessed wrong.
- **Team or Only me:** 👥 Team = everyone sees it. 🔒 Only me = others see a grey **Busy** block, with no title, client or place. Hidden work events still count in your numbers. Personal events never count.
- **After the appointment:** tap **✓ Done**, **💰 Closed**, or **✕ Didn't happen**. Only confirmed events count. The app never guesses. At 9pm it reminds you if anything is still unconfirmed.
- **Not sure if the client will sign?** Tap **✓ Done** on the presentation or follow-up. If they sign later, open the same event and tap **💰 Closed**. Add **one line per policy**:
  - **Plan name**, as on the proposal
  - **ANP** (yearly premium, e.g. RM300 monthly = RM3,600)
  - **Payment mode:** M / Q / HY / Y

  Tap **+ Add another case** for more policies. NOC = number of lines and total ANP = their sum; average case size = ANP ÷ NOC. ANP can be left blank until you know; the case still counts. The whole team sees your plan names, ANP and mode in the close case list (never client details).
- **With:** tag a leader or teammate for joint field work. They see it on their calendar and get a pop-up.
- **👥 Everyone's in it:** switch this on for the team meeting, training or a roadshow. It shows on every member's calendar.
- **Cheer 👏 and comment** on any event. There's no separate chat; all talk happens in comments. When someone closes a case, the whole team gets a 🎉 pop-up.
- **Who gets a comment pop-up:**
  - **Whole-team event:** every member.
  - **Anyone else's event:** only the owner (plus anyone tagged on it). When the owner replies, only the people who commented get it. Nobody else is disturbed.
  - Under every comment box, the app shows exactly who will be notified.
- **🔔 Comments tab:** every comment and cheer meant for you, newest first, with an unread count.

## Team events (meeting, training, roadshow)
- Create the event with **👥 Everyone's in it** switched on. It appears on everyone's calendar and everyone gets a pop-up asking **Going?**
- Open the event and tap **✅ Going** or **❌ Can't go**. The card lists who is going, who can't, and who hasn't replied. On **Team day** each person's row shows ✅ / ❌ / ⏳.
- **1 hour before**, everyone gets a pop-up with the count, e.g. "✅ 8 going, ❌ 2 can't, ⏳ 3 no reply".

## Leaders
- **Calendar → Team day:** one row per person for the day. Grey = private busy, blank = free. The top line says who is **free right now**. Tap a person's event and leave a comment to set a time; only they get the pop-up.
- **Activity tab:** your team board (A / O / P / F / Recruit, **Total activities**, NOC, ANP, average case, NOC/P, O/A, P/O, F/P, unconfirmed, last seen), with week, last week, month and last month. Open a closed event to see its plan names, modes and ANP.
  - **Needs a check-in** lists agents with no confirmed selling activity in 5 days.
  - **📋 Copy WhatsApp report** pastes straight into your group. **⬇ CSV** opens in Excel.
- **Me → Manage members:** make someone a leader, or remove someone who has left. Removing signs them out everywhere, and their events stay.

**Everyone** sees the team totals at the top of the board (total activities, NOC, ANP, average case), each agent's **NOC and ANP**, and a **Team total** row. Ratios (O/A, P/O, F/P, NOC/P), average case per agent, unconfirmed counts and last seen are for leaders only.

**Close case list** (bottom of the Activity tab, for everyone): one line per policy in date order, then the total, in your WhatsApp format. Tap **📋 Copy** and paste it into the group. It follows the period you picked (this week / last week / this month / last month). Example:

    🔥NOVEMBER CLOSE CASE LIST🔥

    1. 1/11 Rex GMS 1198/M
    2. 3/11 Wen GMS 1507.65/Y

    Total ANP: 2,705.65

The name is the agent's first name as they signed up, so members should sign up with the name the team uses. A case with no ANP yet shows as "ANP?" and isn't added to the total.

## Moving an old calendar in
- **Photos or screenshots** (Google Calendar, TimeTree, iPhone, Samsung, WhatsApp, or a handwritten diary): **Me → Import → Choose photos**, up to 10 at a time. AI reads them, then you check the list:
  - **Green** rows are clear and already ticked.
  - **Red** rows have an unclear date or time. Fix them and tick them, or leave them out. Red rows are never added on their own.
  - Events already in your calendar are skipped, so importing the same photo twice is safe.
- **.ics file** (exact copy, no AI): Google Calendar on a computer → Settings → Import & export → Export.

Photos are read and then thrown away; they are never saved. Crop out client IC or phone numbers first if they're visible.

## Pop-ups
WhatsApp-style banner with sound for: comments meant for you (rules above), cheers on your events, a teammate's 🎉 close, being tagged or added to a whole-team event, a reminder before each event (default 30 minutes, can be changed per event), an 8am "today" summary, and a 9pm "confirm" nudge.
