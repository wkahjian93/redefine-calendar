# REDEFINE Calendar: how to use

**The problem it fixes:** you had to ask each agent one by one when they're busy and what they're doing. Now everyone's schedule and activity is on one calendar you can open any time, and private things stay private.

## First time (every member, 1 minute)
1. Open the app link. On iPhone: Safari → Share ⬆︎ → **Add to Home Screen**. On Android: Chrome ⋮ → **Install app**.
2. Type the **team code**, your **name** and a **4–6 digit PIN**. Leaders use the **leader code** instead.
3. **Me → Turn on notifications** (iPhone: open the app from the home screen first).
4. **Me → Import calendar** to move your old appointments in (see below).

**Set your short name:** Me → **My short name** (e.g. KJ, MJ, Rex). The team sees it on chips, Team day, attendance and the close case list, so no surnames get mixed up. Until you set it, the app suggests your given-name initials (WONG KAH JIAN → KJ).

You stay signed in on that phone for good, like WhatsApp. Only two things sign you out: tapping **Sign out** on that phone, or a leader removing you. Changing the team code or losing signal never does.

## Every day (agents)
- **Add an appointment:** tap **+**, type what it is ("Present Mr Lim medical card", "签单 Ooi", "FU Jason"). The app works out the type (👋 Approach, 📊 Present, 🔁 Follow-up, 🤝 Join field, 🌱 Recruit, 👥 Meeting, 🛠️ Service, 🎁 CRM, 📝 Paperwork, 🏠 Personal). Tap another type if it guessed wrong. There is no Closing type: a signing meeting ("签单 Ooi") is a 🔁 Follow-up, and you record the sale with **💰 Closed** after it. Older Closing events still show and count.
- **Team or Only me:** 👥 Team = everyone sees it. 🔒 Only me = others see a grey **Busy** block, with no title, client or place. Hidden work events still count in your numbers. Personal events never count.
- **After the appointment:** tap **✓ Done**, **💰 Closed**, or **✕ Didn't happen**. Only confirmed events count. The app never guesses. At 9pm it reminds you if anything is still unconfirmed.
- **Presentations:** setting a 📊 presentation counts **O +1** (an appointment) straight away, so there is no separate Open type any more ("FF" / "1st meet" titles become presentations; older Open events still count). When it happens (✓ Done or 💰 Closed) it also counts **P +1**. If it didn't happen, O stays and P doesn't count. The app then offers **📅 Postpone**: pick the new date and time, and it moves the same appointment (O isn't counted twice), notes the old date, and tells anyone invited. The presentation page also has a **📅 Postpone to a new date** button.
- **Not sure if the client will sign?** Tap **✓ Done** on the presentation or follow-up. If they sign later, open the same event and tap **💰 Closed**. Add **one line per policy**:
  - **Plan name**, as on the proposal
  - **ANP** (yearly premium, e.g. RM300 monthly = RM3,600)
  - **Payment mode:** M / Q / HY / Y

  Tap **+ Add another case** for more policies. NOC = number of lines and total ANP = their sum; average case size = ANP ÷ NOC. ANP can be left blank until you know; the case still counts. The whole team sees your plan names, ANP and mode in the close case list (never client details).
- **🤝 Join field (陪访):** pick the Join field type, then say whether it is a **📊 Presentation** or a **🔁 Follow-up**, and who the **Agent** is (whose client it is). Everyone else tagged is the leader going along. The app picks the agent for you: if an agent sets it, it is them; if a leader sets it, it is the agent they tag. All the numbers (O, P, F) and any closed cases count **only for the agent**, never for the leader. The agent can tap ✓ Done / 💰 Closed and key in the cases themselves, even when the leader set the event. When a leader sets it, the agent is in it straight away (no accept / reject) and gets a pop-up.
- **With:** tag a leader or teammate for joint field work. They see it on their calendar and get a pop-up.
- **👥 Everyone's in it:** switch this on for the team meeting, training or a roadshow. It shows on every member's calendar.
- **Cheer 👏 and comment** on any event. There's no separate chat; all talk happens in comments. When someone closes a case, the whole team gets a 🎉 pop-up.
- **Who gets a comment pop-up:**
  - **Whole-team event:** every member.
  - **Anyone else's event:** only the owner (plus anyone tagged on it). When the owner replies, only the people who commented get it. Nobody else is disturbed.
  - Under every comment box, the app shows exactly who will be notified.
- **🔔 Comments tab:** every comment and cheer meant for you, newest first, with an unread count.

## Planning tools (app version 28)
- **🔁 Repeat:** in a new event, choose Every week / month / year and an optional *Until* date. To change or delete a repeating event, open it on the date you want and tap ✏️ Edit (or Delete): the app asks **Only this date** or **Every repeat**. Only this date leaves every other date as it was. ✓ Done / 💰 Closed are marked on the first one only; repeat copies never count in your numbers (a date you changed on its own becomes a normal event that counts).
- **📄 Duplicate:** open any event and tap **📄 Duplicate**. A new event opens with the same title, type, place, notes, people and time; just change the date or time and tap Add.
- **Holidays:** Malaysia + Penang public holidays (red date), festivals, and school holidays (light blue days) show on the calendar. You get a pop-up the day before and on the day.
- **🎂 Birthdays:** Me → **My birthday** (day + month only) puts it on everyone's calendar with a pop-up that morning. Me → **Important people** adds yearly birthdays and anniversaries for family or clients (🔒 only you by default).
- **✅ To-do tab:** 4 boxes: 🔴 urgent & important, 🟠 urgent, 🔵 important, ⚪ neither. **Tap a box** to add a to-do straight into it (with optional details and a date). **Hold a box’s 🎤** and speak: what you say is written down and saved in that box. **Tap a to-do** to edit it, add details, change its date or move it to another box, or delete it. Everything is on one list; finished ones stay for a week (tap *Show older finished ones* for the rest).
- **📝 Notes (To-do tab → Notes):** tap **＋ New note** to write, or **hold 🎤 Hold to talk** and speak to save a note as text. Tap a note to edit or delete it. To-dos and notes are private. Each voice recording counts towards the same daily limit as voice booking, and recordings are not kept.
- **🎯 Goals (Activity tab):** set an ANP goal for the month, quarter, half-year or year. The app works out how many approaches, appointments, presentations and closes you need **each week**, from your own last 90 days (or the team's if you're new). **📄 Monthly report** compares you with the team average and adds a coach's note.
- **📖 Tour:** Me → Take the tour, any time.
- **Agenda** shows one week, Monday at the top and Sunday at the bottom.
- **Swipe:** on the calendar, swipe left for the next month (next week in Agenda, next day in Team day, anywhere on the people grid), swipe right for the previous one. The page follows your finger and the next one slides in beside it; let go early and it springs back.

## More (app version 29)
- **🎤 Voice booking:** tap the 🎤 button above **+** and say the appointment in any mix of English, 华语, Cantonese, Hokkien or Malay, e.g. "Esok 3 点 present medical card to Mr Lim at Gurney". Up to 1 minute. If the app heard what, the date and the time, it saves straight away (with **Undo**). If something is missing it opens the form with what it heard, and you fill in the rest. Hokkien is understood less well than the other languages. Each recording counts towards the daily photo-reading limit, and recordings are not kept.
- **Several appointments in one voice note:** just say them one after another, e.g. "Esok 3pm present Mr Lim at Gurney, Friday 10am coffee Ali, and training Monday and Wednesday 8pm". Each one is saved separately (the same thing on several dates becomes one appointment per date), up to 10 per note. Each is marked "one of N from this voice note" in the voice entries to check.
- **Microphone question:** iPhone asks "Allow microphone?" the first time you use 🎤 after opening the app. Tap **Allow**. The app then keeps the microphone ready (iPhone may show a small mic dot) so it won't ask again until you leave the app or don't record for 5 minutes. Nothing is recorded unless you hold 🎤.
- **📅 Calendar subscription (Me):** shows your appointments in Apple Calendar or Google Calendar, so their Home Screen widgets show what's next. The setup steps are in the card. Keep adding and changing appointments in this app; the other calendar picks up changes when it refreshes (Apple: as often as you set it; Google: usually every few hours, up to a day). TimeTree can't subscribe to a link. **The link shows all your appointments, private ones too:** don't share it, and tap **New link** if you did.
- **🎵 Music & animations (Me, per device):** light, cheerful background music (轻快) is **off** until you switch it on. Celebration animations can be switched off too.
- **All your devices:** phone, iPad and laptop show the same data, which is kept on the server. Deleting the app or changing phone loses nothing: sign in again with your name and PIN. Kept "Me + …" chips also follow you. Only the music and animation switches are per device.

## Celebration themes (owner only)
- **Me → Celebration themes → Manage themes.** Only the owner sees this. The owner is the first leader who joined, unless the Script property `OWNER_NAME` names someone else.
- **New theme from a poster:**
  1. Choose the poster, then tap **✨ Read the poster with AI**.
  2. The AI fills in the occasion, colours, the pictures on the poster (as emoji) and an animation (snow, balloons, lion dance + firecrackers, fireworks…).
  3. Check the dates (suggested for each festival), add music if you like (MP3/M4A, up to 5 MB), tap **👀 Preview**, then **Save**.
- **Switch on** at the top turns themes on for everyone. **Switch off** brings everyone back to the normal look.
- On the dates you set, everyone's app takes the theme's colours, shows the poster faintly behind the calendar with a ribbon on top, and plays the animation. The app keeps words readable even when a colour is too light or dark. A **Team birthdays** theme appears on any member's birthday.

## Inviting teammates to your event
- On the event form, pick people under **Invite**. They get a pop-up: "Alicia invited you 📩 … Accept or reject?"
- On their calendar the event shows **faded** with "📩 Invited: tap to answer", and a **📩 invitations waiting** bar lists them.
- **✅ Accept**: the event turns full colour. **❌ Reject**: it disappears from their calendar (they can still accept later). You, the owner, get a pop-up either way.
- The event shows everyone's answer: ✅ accepted, ⏳ not answered, ❌ rejected. Everyone still on the event can comment and gets comment pop-ups; whoever rejected is left out.

## Partner calendar (husband & wife)
- **Me → Partner calendar** → choose your partner → **Send request**. Your partner opens **Me** and taps **Accept**. Nothing is shared until both agree.
- Once linked: a **💑 Me + partner** chip on the calendar shows both calendars together, and you each see the other's **private** events in full. Everyone else (leaders included) still sees Busy. Each person's activity numbers stay their own.
- **Unlink** any time in the same card.
- **See one or several people with you:** tap the **Me + …** chip, tick one or more people and tap **Show together**: your events and theirs show on one calendar (their private events stay Busy). On that chip tap **📌** to keep it for next time, or **×** to close it.
- **Your own order of people:** press and hold a person's chip, slide it left or right, and let go. The order is saved on all your devices. After you tap a chip, the row stays where you scrolled it.

## Team events (meeting, training, roadshow)
- Create the event with **👥 Everyone's in it** switched on. It appears on everyone's calendar and everyone gets a pop-up asking **Going?**
- Open the event and tap **✅ Going** or **❌ Can't go**. The card lists who is going, who can't, and who hasn't replied. On **Team day** each person's row shows ✅ / ❌ / ⏳.
- **1 hour before**, everyone gets a pop-up with the count, e.g. "✅ 8 going, ❌ 2 can't, ⏳ 3 no reply".

## Leaders
- **Calendar → Team day:** one row per person for the day. Grey = private busy, blank = free. The top line says who is **free right now**. Tap a person's event and leave a comment to set a time; only they get the pop-up.
- **Activity tab:** your team board (A / O / P / F / Recruit, **Total activities**, NOC, ANP, average case, NOC/P, O/A, P/O, F/P, unconfirmed, last seen), with week, last week, month and last month. Open a closed event to see its plan names, modes and ANP.
  - **Needs a check-in** lists agents with no confirmed selling activity in 5 days.
  - **📋 Copy WhatsApp report** pastes straight into your group. **⬇ CSV** opens in Excel.
- **Me → Manage members:** make someone a leader, or remove someone who has left. Removing signs them out everywhere, and their events stay.
- **🔎 Best time for a team activity:**
  1. Go to Calendar → Upcoming team events → **Find the time most people are free**.
  2. Pick how long, when (weekday nights / weekends / daytime / any) and how far ahead.
  3. The app lists the best slots: how many people are free, who has something booked, and who is *usually* busy then. Usual means their pattern over the last 8 weeks, so a night that only looks free because nothing is booked yet is ranked lower. Private appointments count as busy without showing details. People with fewer than 5 appointments in 8 weeks aren't counted (they're named).
  4. **📣 Set team activity here** opens a whole-team event already filled in.
  
  The table at the bottom shows each weekday's usual free time.
- **Forgot PIN:** Me → Manage members → **🔑 Reset PIN** on that person. Tell them the 6-digit temporary PIN (shown once). They sign in with it and must choose their own PIN straight away. Their other phones stay signed in.

**Everyone** sees the team totals at the top of the board (total activities, NOC, ANP, average case), each agent's **NOC and ANP**, and a **Team total** row. Ratios (O/A, P/O, F/P, NOC/P), average case per agent, unconfirmed counts and last seen are for leaders only.

**Close case list** (bottom of the Activity tab, for everyone): one line per policy in date order, then the total, in your WhatsApp format. Tap **📋 Copy** and paste it into the group. It follows the period you picked (this week / last week / this month / last month). Example:

    🔥NOVEMBER CLOSE CASE LIST🔥

    1. 1/11 Rex GMS 1198/M
    2. 3/11 Wen GMS 1507.65/Y

    Total ANP: 2,705.65

The name is the agent's first name as they signed up, so members should sign up with the name the team uses. A case with no ANP yet shows as "ANP?" and isn't added to the total.

## Moving an old calendar in
- **Type or paste words:** Me → Import → **Type or paste words**. Paste a WhatsApp message, notes or a list, or just type (e.g. "Tomorrow 3pm present Mr Lim at Gurney, Sat 10am coffee Ali"), then tap **✨ Add from words**. The AI finds each appointment: clear ones are added straight away, and anything without a clear date or time is shown for you to fix. Pasting the same words again adds nothing new.
- **Photos or screenshots** (Google Calendar, TimeTree, iPhone, Samsung, WhatsApp, or a handwritten diary): **Me → Import → Choose photos**, up to 10 at a time. AI reads them, then you check the list:
  - **Green** rows are clear and already ticked.
  - **Red** rows have an unclear date or time. Fix them and tick them, or leave them out. Red rows are never added on their own.
  - Events already in your calendar are skipped, so importing the same photo twice is safe, even if the AI reads it slightly differently the second time.
  - After you tap **Add**, a progress box stays on screen until it is done, so it can’t be sent twice. If the signal drops, the app checks what already went in and unticks those.
  - **CGBD 7-11 weekly diary:** photograph one week page at a time (sideways is fine). The app reads the date range at the top, the day columns and the 7am–11pm rows; the O/P/F/R boxes and the review box are ignored.
- **.ics file** (exact copy, no AI): Google Calendar on a computer → Settings → Import & export → Export.

Photos are read and then thrown away; they are never saved. Crop out client IC or phone numbers first if they're visible.

## Pop-ups
WhatsApp-style banner with sound for: comments meant for you (rules above), cheers on your events, a teammate's 🎉 close, being tagged or added to a whole-team event, a reminder before each event (default 30 minutes, can be changed per event), an 8am "today" summary, and a 9pm "confirm" nudge.
